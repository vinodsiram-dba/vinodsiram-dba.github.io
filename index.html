About Me

Engineering databases that never sleep

I'm Venkat Vinod Kumar Siram, a Senior Oracle DBA and Database Engineer with over 20 years of experience in Oracle database administration, software development and system architecture.

My work spans database design, high availability, disaster recovery, performance tuning and replication — keeping mission-critical systems running for some of the largest retail, payments, banking and telecom businesses in the US. Today I help Sephora run its warehouse and supply-chain databases across RAC, Data Guard, GoldenGate and OCI.

I enjoy turning complex data problems into reliable, well-documented solutions, and I'm proud to have been recognised as best performer for migrating 65 million user records during a major application upgrade at Sephora.

What I Do

Core Expertise

From multi-node clusters to cross-cloud replication, I build and run database platforms that stay fast, secure and available.

Oracle RAC & Exadata

4-node 11g–19c RAC on RHEL with ASM, adding nodes, converting single-instance databases to cluster, and Exadata management and monitoring.

High Availability & DR

Active Data Guard with flashback, cascaded and multi-site standbys, FSFO with DG Broker, switchover/failover, gap resolution and standby rebuilds.

GoldenGate & Replication

GoldenGate install, upgrade and tuning; active-active, bi-directional, heterogeneous and DDL replication; Veridata; Shareplex and DataMirror.

Performance Tuning

AWR, ASH, ADDM and SQLT analysis, Explain Plan and SQL Trace, partitioning strategies, and automated execution-plan comparison across upgrades.

Cloud & Migration

On-prem to OCI migrations via GoldenGate Marketplace, AWS EC2 databases, upgrades from 11g/12c to 18c/19c, Windows-to-Linux and SQL Server-to-Oracle migrations.

Automation & Security

Shell scripting and cron automation, OEM 13c patching, RMAN backup strategies, TDE and DBMS_CRYPTO encryption, roles, profiles and SOPs.
